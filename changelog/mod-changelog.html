----MOD 2.9:
[2026/09/08]
The Catacombs are reachable. 26 finished locations — The Mourning Hall, Eleven Wisemen,
  Thawing Candles, Tunnel of the Dead — written by the game's author and linked to by nothing
  An entrance on the Village Center, which is where their own exit already led
  Not a word of it is the mod's; the entrance is the whole change
  Opens once you have been to the deep forest, so the level cap ladder keeps its order:
  forest 20 → deep forest 30 → catacombs 40 → golem arena 50
  This also makes the cap-40 rung live for the first time — nothing could reach it before
The Pill Tower. The author wrote this choice on the Village Center and commented it out;
  the location behind it was never built. Now it is
  An alchemists' tower that sells the pill grades a village herbalist cannot source,
  realms 6–10 and the Sublime and Transcendent Spirit Pills
  A spirit vein you can sit in once a day for Qi Circulation exp, scaling with your realm
  They do not let mortals past the door — you need a realm to get in

----MOD 2.8:
[2026/09/08]
FIXED: the breakthrough pills had no source. All ten existed as items and nothing gave,

Three sources now, matching how far along each realm is:
  Realm 1 — the instructor hands it over when the dojo finishes teaching you
  Realms 2–5 — the Herbalist, who already sells spirit pills (900 to 38,000 copper)
  Realms 6–10 — the dojo's Level Advancement rungs at 45, 60, 75, 90 and 105

  Paper Boy, a 40% chance per visit, and realm 1 opens long before that
The Herbalist also stocks the Superior and Refined Spirit Pills now

----MOD 2.6:
[2026/09/08]
Cultivation realms. The game had Qi Circulation, spirit pills "made from condensed Ki",
  meridians in its skill text — and no realm to advance through. Now there are seven:
  Ten realms above Mortal, on exactly the levels that earn each title rank — so realm 3
  is the Qi Circulation level that earns a rank 3 title, and its own title comes out rank 3
  Qi Refining · Foundation Establishment · Core Formation · Nascent Soul · Spirit Severing ·
  Soul Transformation · Void Refinement · Body Integration · Great Ascension · Tribulation Transcendence
  Reaching the Qi Circulation level does NOT advance you — it puts you at a bottleneck
  A breakthrough costs a pill, and can fail; training past the requirement improves the odds
  Each realm multiplies every stat and widens what the body holds, up to x6 at the top
  Ten breakthrough pills, one per realm, and a title for each realm reached
Elemental mastery. The six Absorption skills were purely defensive — trained by being hit,
  and no way to use an element at all. Six Mastery skills to match them:
  Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Light and Dark, each with a technique that fires in combat
  "Lantern Splitting Palm", "Still Water Draw", "Hollow Gale", "Settling Weight",
  "Noon Without Shade", "Lamp Going Out"
  They scale with INT and their element rather than with your weapon, and are capped at
  45% of strikes so a weapon still matters
  Nothing fires below Qi Refining — your channels are not open yet
modRealm() and modMastery() print the ladders

----MOD 2.3:
[2026/09/08]
Title ranks now run 1–10 and are derived from the level that earns them, not assigned by feel
  rank 1 at lv 1, rank 3 at 18, rank 5 at 45, rank 7 at 70, rank 9 at 90, rank 10 at 110
  (the base game gave out eight rank-3 titles at skill level 8–15 — Runner at 10, Rookie at 15)
Every skill now grants five titles, at levels 25 / 50 / 75 / 90 / 110 — ranks 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 10
  465 new titles; the pool goes from 120 to 585, and all ten ranks are populated
Titles do something now: each raises its own skill's exp rate, 5% at rank 3 up to 30% at rank 10
  Only the title you are WEARING applies — which finally gives the selection screen a purpose
  At renown level N, every title of rank N or lower applies permanently whether worn or not,
  so renown 10 makes the whole collection passive
Renown thresholds rescaled to the larger pool: level 10 now wants 550 titles rather than 100
modTitles() prints held/total by rank and which ranks have gone passive; modTitles(n) lists one rank
The title screen groups by skill — one row showing the best you hold, with a caret to open the rest
  Base-game titles group with the generated ones, so Rookie and Fighter sit under Fighting too
  Story titles have no skill and stay as plain rows below the groups
Titles are coloured by rank, in the picker and in the tooltip, and the tooltip states the rank
  grey 1, cyan 2, lime 3, yellow 4, orange 5, purple 6, pink 7, red 8, gold 9, white 10
  FIXED: the game's own rank 7 tooltip threw an error (it set `this.dl`, which is undefined
  in that branch). It had never run because the base game had no title above rank 5 —
  there are now 93 at rank 7. Ranks 1, 8, 9 and 10 had no colour at all

----MOD 2.2:
[2026/09/08]
Perks now run the whole ladder — every skill has milestones at 10/25/50/60/75/90/110
  (447 added; 81 of 94 skills previously gave nothing at all between level 51 and 110)
Perk rewards match the skill's type: resistances give resistance, absorptions give elemental defence,
  combat gives STR and crit damage, mental gives INT and EXP, gathering gives energy
Critical damage from combat perks kept deliberately small — it is shared by 13 skills and compounds,
  and at full value a crit reached 23x a normal swing, which made combat a coin flip on crits
The four added actions are earned now, not handed over on load
  Toughness 4 → Endurance Drill, Harvesting 4 → Forage,
  Temperance 5 → Circulate Qi, Literacy 8 → Practice Calligraphy
New settings checkbox "Unrestricted actions": run several actions at once, and start them anywhere
  (off by default — both are limits the base game means to impose)
Three save slots, with a "saves" panel and a "new save" button in the bottom bar
  Switching saves the slot you are leaving first; "delete the save" now deletes only the slot you are in
Changelog reachable from a "changelog" button in the bottom bar, not just the version number

----MOD 2.1:
[2026/09/08]
Enemy scaling rewritten — it had been scaling the wrong fields
  An enemy's STR is subtracted from your damage, so scaling it armoured them instead of
  lengthening the fight. The tutorial needed ~63,000 swings and killed you in one;
  everything from the southern forest onward was kill-in-1 / die-in-1
Difficulty is now solved per spawn against your actual power: ~8 swings to kill, ~20 to die
Enemies in an area's higher level band are genuinely harder, and each creature keeps its own character
Arena golems are proper boss fights rather than the softest thing in the game

----MOD 2.0:
[2026/09/07]
The ~100 "discovered by playing" skills consolidated to 10, with the same total effect
Skill EXP curve replaced so level 110 is reachable in about six months at 1x speed
  (the base game's curve made level 109→110 alone cost 1.16e14 xp)
The three added areas now stay hidden until golem arena IV is cleared, then open in order
Saves from before this update still load; skills that were merged away simply do not return
