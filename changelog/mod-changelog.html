----MOD 3.3:
[2026/09/15]
Fixed: every title was listed twice after loading a save.
  Found by auditing, not reported. giveTitle pushes each earned title onto TWO arrays —
  global.titles and global.titlese — and load() rebuilds global.titles from the save
  BY INDEX and then appends the whole of titlese on top. So everything earned that
  session ends up in the array twice. Measured on a character holding 241 titles: 241
  before a load, 481 after, 241 distinct.
  It does not compound — the next load finds titlese empty — but one load is enough,
  and save() writes the inflated array straight back out
  Both title screens are affected: the game's own, and the mod's grouped picker. Both
  iterate global.titles, so each title got two rows
  A base-game bug, but the mod is what makes it loud: vanilla hands out around a hundred
  titles and section 24 grants five per skill, so the doubling is hundreds of rows.
  Existing saves are repaired on load, by identity, the same way the skill sheet is —
  global.titles holds references to the objects in ttl, so the same object twice is
  always wrong whatever put it there. Not fixed at the source: titlese is the author's
  mechanism and the mod has no business changing what it means
Hardened: the rank-duel scaler now refuses the player and creature.default explicitly,
  matching MOD_scaleEnemy, instead of relying on their not carrying a spawn target
Tests. tests/ids.mjs gains 8 checks for the title list: distinct before a load, distinct
  after one, distinct after two, no holes, the worn title still present, and an
  already-doubled list repaired. Verified they fail without the fix (85 entries for 43
  distinct) and pass with it
Audit, no change needed. All 75 locations draw without throwing, no choice row wraps its
  22px line, all 12 wiki pages build, no id clashes in any namespace, the wiki has no
  horizontal overflow at 375/768/1200px, no interval leaks over 15s at 20x speed, and
  acts/you.skls/furn/qsts all stay distinct across repeated save/load cycles

----MOD 3.2:
[2026/09/11]
Rank 1 is reachable. It just never felt like it.
  The number under your portrait is the base game's Power rank, and it is DERIVED from
  your stats every time it is drawn — there is no stored field. Measured on a character
  built to each rung of the ladder: rank 21,617,349,975 at tier 0, 65,544 at tier 5, 258 at
  tier 7, 20 at tier 8, and 1 at the very top. Ranks 1–10 all live inside the last fifteen
  skill levels — at 108 across the board you are still rank 3 — after a whole game in
  which the number is an unreadable ten-digit blob.
So there is a second way to hold a rank: take it off whoever has it.
  The Hall of the First Gate, off the Old Path trailhead past the Western Woods gate.
  Ten named challengers, rank 10 down to rank 1. Beating one takes that rank
  Strictly in order. Rank N opens only when rank N+1 has been taken, and each rung also
  wants a character level (60, 65, 70 ... 100, 110), so the ten fights span the endgame
  instead of being one evening
  The rank you hold floors the rank you are shown, and never caps it — hold rank 6 with
  rank-900 stats and it shows 6; pass it on your own and it shows the better number. A held
  rank is painted gold
  Implemented by wrapping you.rank and keeping the earned rank in global.flags, like
  mod_realm. Nothing is written into a stat, so there is no delta to track and nothing
  compounds on load — the trap you.res and skl.p both fell into.
  Losing costs nothing but the walk back. A rank you have not taken cannot be taken off
  you, and a rank you have taken is never lost
The duels get longer, not deadlier. Each is a protected one-creature area, so the enemy
  scaling already treats it as an arena boss and anchors it to your power as you walk in.
  Each rung then steps up ~7% into FIGHT LENGTH — the rank 1 duel runs 1.77x the rank 10
  one — and the kill<die guarantee is re-derived from the targets afterwards rather than
  hoped for
  Scaling both targets in opposite directions does not survive ten rungs: margin 1.5 over
  step^1.5 is already under 1.2 by a step of 1.16.
Wiki. A new "The rank ladder" page, after Progression: the formula, what a maxed
  character actually measures at, and the ten rungs with their gates and payouts
Found, not fixed: the player's real max HP is not the one the balance model uses.
  Section 9's hpTrack assigns you.hpmax at the end of the allbuff wrapper, but allbuff
  opens with stat_r(), which recomputes hpmax from the base — so the raised value only
  survives until the next stat_r, and the tick calls that constantly. Sampled 43 times over
  six seconds of real play on a cap-60 character, you.hpmax was 10,050 every time; the
  hpTrack value of 184,754 never appeared. Every balance script reads it in the one instant
  it is standing, so enemy damage is sized for ~18x the health you really have. Harmless
  early, worst in the endgame. Written up in MOD-NOTES and CLAUDE.md; the fix is its own
  job, because it moves a number all 2,790 matchups are measured against.
Tests. tests/ranks.mjs — 31 checks. The wrapper must floor and never cap; the held rank
  must survive a save/load; the hall must offer exactly one rung at a time and every line
  must fit a 22px row; and all 100 matchups (10 rungs x 10 story tiers) must keep kill<die
  with zero whiffs, measured through the game's own dmg_calc. allareas is now 2,790
  matchups across 31 areas

Fixed: a hundred Fire Masteries and a hundred Companions Disciplines.
  Two skills shared id 2010 — the Companions parent (2000 + section 10,
  section 11) and Fire Mastery (2010 + 0, section 29). Ids are the save's key for a
  skill and the loader matches them in a loop with no break, so one shared id pushed
  BOTH skills onto the sheet for BOTH saved entries. The next save wrote all of them.
  It doubled every save/load: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32... A week of play is a hundred of each.
  Only Fire Mastery moved (2010 → 2016), so water through dark keep the ids they were
  saved under and nobody loses those levels
  The Companions parent gets id 2010 back, and with it the level cap that the clash had
  been handing to Fire Mastery instead
Existing saves are repaired. The duplicates are IN the save, so fixing the id only stopped
  it getting worse. The skill list is now de-duplicated on load and the panel redrawn —
  a 138-row sheet comes back as 12, with every level intact
Also fixed: The Sunken Hollow and the Pill Tower both had location id 976 (the mod's own,
  from MOD 2.9). global.lst_loc is matched the same way, so both would have drawn.
  The tower is 980 now
Tests. tests/ids.mjs — 32 checks. Every namespace (skl, ttl, act, abl, item, wpn, eqp,
  sld, acc, chss, area, creature, quest, rcp) must have distinct ids; five save/load cycles
  must not grow the sheet; and a deliberately damaged sheet must come back clean.
  Nothing checked this before, which is why it shipped.

You can get into the Marketplace again.
  The base game's chain: finish at the dojo, then a Paper Boy turns up at the village
  center at 40% a visit with a "Pamphlet", then you read it for three hours. Exactly one
  item in the game sets the flag, and the Paper Boy is retired the moment the Pamphlet is
  GIVEN rather than read. MOD 1.x added selling and never marked it a key item — so you
  could sell it at the food stand, before ever reading it, and lose the marketplace, its
  three shops, the guard-duty quest and the realm 2-5 breakthrough pills permanently.
  Key items can no longer be sold. Derived, not listed: the game's own source is scanned
  for flags it ever sets back to false, and an item that sets a flag nothing clears is
  the only copy of something. Six items protected. A Smoke Bomb sets a flag the game does
  clear, so it is state rather than a key, and stays sellable
  The Paper Boy comes back if you no longer have a Pamphlet — which is what gets a save
  that is already stuck out of the hole, without a console. A fresh character still meets
  him exactly once, through the author's own code
  And the village center says WHY the door is not there instead of silently omitting the
  line: "finish at the dojo first", "read your Pamphlet", or "you do not know the way",
  with the full explanation in the tooltip. The Herbalist inside gets the same treatment
  Added lines sit above the location's "<= Return" and are kept under 50 characters:
  .chs is height:22px with no overflow rule, so a choice that wraps spills into the next row.
Tests. tests/marketplace.mjs — 30 checks, including that every added line fits on one row.

----MOD 3.0:
[2026/09/09]
A wiki. A wiki button on the bottom bar and a "Game wiki" row in settings, both
  opening a reference in a tab of its own. Eleven pages: Start here, Progression, Skills,
  Cultivation, Elemental mastery, Titles, Areas & enemies, Items, Actions, The dojo, Mechanics
  It is GENERATED from the live game data every time it is opened, not written out.
  A hand-written wiki for 100 skills, 595 titles, 371 items and 19 areas would be wrong
  within a week, and a reference nobody can trust is worse than none. Reading skl, item,
  area, ttl, act and the mod's own tables means it cannot disagree with the game —
  including changes made from the console mid-session.
  It reads the save too, so it doubles as a progress sheet: your level in every skill,
  which perks you have taken, which titles you hold, your realm and what the next one costs
  Everything you have not reached is still listed — it is a wiki, not a fog-of-war map
  Groups collapse. With everything listed, three pages ran past 40,000 pixels open;
  collapsed, the tallest is 5,800 and Skills opens as ten lines you can take in at once
  Search opens the groups that still have matches, since a match hidden inside a
  collapsed group reads as no match at all
  No fetches and no dependencies — it works from file:// as readily as from a server
Two things found by generating it rather than writing it:
  Spawn chances were not what pop[i].c says. z_bake normalises those weights into
  area.popc, the [lo,hi] bands mon_gen actually rolls against, and the weights need not
  sum to 1. The Southern forest's .35/.45/.25 are really 33/43/24%. The wiki reads the bands
  Nothing can ever spawn in the Damp cellar (base game). Its pop entries carry no
  weight at all, so z_bake produces NaN bands and no comparison in the spawn loop can be
  true. Nothing in the game travels there either. Left as it is and reported in the wiki
  rather than quietly fixed — it is the author's unfinished content, like chss.pltwr1 was
Not listed as places: "Somewhere" (where current_z parks whenever you are not fighting)
and "Test" (reached only from chss.tst, which has id -1 and is in no sector).
Tests. tests/wiki.mjs — 57 checks. Coverage is counted from the game rather than
  listed, so adding a skill without adding it to the wiki fails the suite automatically
