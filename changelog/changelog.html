----MOD 2.3:
[2026/09/08]
Title ranks now run 1–10 and are derived from the level that earns them, not assigned by feel
  rank 1 at lv 1, rank 3 at 18, rank 5 at 45, rank 7 at 70, rank 9 at 90, rank 10 at 110
  (the base game gave out eight rank-3 titles at skill level 8–15 — Runner at 10, Rookie at 15)
Every skill now grants five titles, at levels 25 / 50 / 75 / 90 / 110 — ranks 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 10
  465 new titles; the pool goes from 120 to 585, and all ten ranks are populated
Titles do something now: each raises its own skill's exp rate, 5% at rank 3 up to 30% at rank 10
  Only the title you are WEARING applies — which finally gives the selection screen a purpose
  At renown level N, every title of rank N or lower applies permanently whether worn or not,
  so renown 10 makes the whole collection passive
Renown thresholds rescaled to the larger pool: level 10 now wants 550 titles rather than 100
modTitles() prints held/total by rank and which ranks have gone passive; modTitles(n) lists one rank

----MOD 2.2:
[2026/09/08]
Perks now run the whole ladder — every skill has milestones at 10/25/50/60/75/90/110
  (447 added; 81 of 94 skills previously gave nothing at all between level 51 and 110)
Perk rewards match the skill's type: resistances give resistance, absorptions give elemental defence,
  combat gives STR and crit damage, mental gives INT and EXP, gathering gives energy
Critical damage from combat perks kept deliberately small — it is shared by 13 skills and compounds,
  and at full value a crit reached 23x a normal swing, which made combat a coin flip on crits
The four added actions are earned now, not handed over on load
  Toughness 4 → Endurance Drill, Harvesting 4 → Forage,
  Temperance 5 → Circulate Qi, Literacy 8 → Practice Calligraphy
New settings checkbox "Unrestricted actions": run several actions at once, and start them anywhere
  (off by default — both are limits the base game means to impose)
Three save slots, with a "saves" panel and a "new save" button in the bottom bar
  Switching saves the slot you are leaving first; "delete the save" now deletes only the slot you are in
Changelog reachable from a "changelog" button in the bottom bar, not just the version number

----MOD 2.1:
[2026/09/08]
Enemy scaling rewritten — it had been scaling the wrong fields
  An enemy's STR is subtracted from your damage, so scaling it armoured them instead of
  lengthening the fight. The tutorial needed ~63,000 swings and killed you in one;
  everything from the southern forest onward was kill-in-1 / die-in-1
Difficulty is now solved per spawn against your actual power: ~8 swings to kill, ~20 to die
Enemies in an area's higher level band are genuinely harder, and each creature keeps its own character
Arena golems are proper boss fights rather than the softest thing in the game

----MOD 2.0:
[2026/09/07]
The ~100 "discovered by playing" skills consolidated to 10, with the same total effect
Skill EXP curve replaced so level 110 is reachable in about six months at 1x speed
  (the base game's curve made level 109→110 alone cost 1.16e14 xp)
The three added areas now stay hidden until golem arena IV is cleared, then open in order
Saves from before this update still load; skills that were merged away simply do not return
